-   4 matzos


-   Soak matzos in water until soft. Drain and squeeze out excess water.


-   6 eggs, separated
-   1/4 cup sugar
-   4 apples, sliced
-   1/2 cup chopped or sliced almonds (optional)
-   1/2 cup raisins (optional)
-   1 Tbsp. cinnamon
-   1/4 lb. (1 stick) butter


-   Mix matzos with beaten egg yolks, sugar, apples, nuts, raisins, and cinnamon.
-   Add butter, melted or chopped into small pieces.


-   6 egg whites


-   Whip egg whites until stiff. Fold gently into apple-matzo mixture.


-   Bake in a well-greased casserole until golden brown on top.

Notes:

<div class="noteMarker" style="float:left">➤</div> The original recipe suggests this can be served plain or "with lemon sauce", whatever that is.
<div class="noteMarker" style="float:left">➤</div> Chop the apples while the matzos soak in the baking dish; dry and grease the baking dish later, while the egg whites are whipping.
