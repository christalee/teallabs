:::: {.steps}

+-----------+-----------------------+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|           | ingredients           | steps                                                                            |
+==========:+:======================+:=================================================================================+
| - 4       | - matzos              | 1. Soak matzos in water until soft. Drain and squeeze out excess water.          |
+-----------+-----------------------+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| - 6       | - eggs,\ separated    | 2. Mix matzos with beaten egg yolks, sugar, apples, nuts, raisins, and cinnamon. |
| - 1/2 cup | - sugar               |                                                                                  |
| - 4       | - apples,\ sliced     |                                                                                  |
| - 1/2 cup | - (almonds),\ chopped |                                                                                  |
| - 1/2 cup | - (raisins)           |                                                                                  |
| - 1 tbsp  | - cinnamon            |                                                                                  |
+-----------+-----------------------+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| - 1/4 cup | - butter (1 stick)    | 3. Mix in butter, melted or chopped into small pieces.                           |
+-----------+-----------------------+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| - 6       | - egg whites          | 4. Whip egg whites until stiff. Fold gently into apple-matzo mixture.            |
|           |                       | 5. Bake in a well-greased casserole until golden brown on top.                   |
+-----------+-----------------------+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+

::::

::: {.notes}
## Notes

- The original recipe suggests this can be served plain or "with lemon sauce", whatever that is.
- Chop the apples while the matzos soak in the baking dish; dry and grease the baking dish later, while the egg whites are whipping.
:::
